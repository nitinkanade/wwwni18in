English grammar explained in simple Marathi allows students to master tense forms and sentence construction rules quickly.

## Sentence Construction Rules (वाक्य रचना)

- **मराठी रचना**: कर्ता + कर्म + क्रियापद (S + O + V)
- **इंग्रजी रचना**: Subject + Verb + Object (S + V + O)
