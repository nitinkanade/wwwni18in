In English grammar, active voice highlights the performer of an action, while passive voice highlights the recipient.

## Core Conversion Rules
1. Move the active object to the subject position.
2. Add appropriate form of `be` verb + V3 (Past Participle).
3. Add preposition `by` before the active agent.
