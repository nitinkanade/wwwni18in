Understanding English tenses is the foundation of spoken and written fluency.

## The 12 English Tenses Chart

| Tense Category | Simple Formula | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Simple Present** | Subject + Verb(s/es) | She writes code every day. |
| **Present Continuous** | Subject + is/am/are + V-ing | She is writing a blog post. |
| **Present Perfect** | Subject + has/have + V3 | She has written three apps. |
| **Present Perfect Cont.** | Subject + has/have been + V-ing | She has been writing since morning. |
