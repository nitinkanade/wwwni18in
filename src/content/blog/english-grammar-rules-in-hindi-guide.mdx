Hindi-medium students often face challenges translating thoughts into correct English sentence structures.

## Basic Sentence Structure Comparison

- **Hindi Pattern**: कर्ता (Subject) + कर्म (Object) + क्रिया (Verb)
- **English Pattern**: Subject + Verb + Object (SVO)

Example:
- *Hindi*: वह (Subject) किताब (Object) पढ़ता है (Verb)।
- *English*: He (Subject) reads (Verb) a book (Object).
