## Overview

**MPSC Quiz Prep in Marathi** is NI18's flagship examination preparation app for candidates appearing for **MPSC (Maharashtra Public Service Commission)** recruitment exams.

### Key Content Coverage

1. **Maharashtra History & Geography**: In-depth coverage of state geography, historical events, and administrative divisions.
2. **Indian Polity & Constitution**: Articles, amendments, and governance modules in Marathi.
3. **General Science & CSAT**: Logical reasoning, quantitative aptitude, and comprehension practice quizzes.
