## Overview

The **JEE Maths Formulas & Revision Companion** by **NI18** is designed specifically for engineering aspirants preparing for **JEE Main & JEE Advanced**.

Built natively with **Kotlin** and **Jetpack Compose**, this lightweight application provides instant access to crucial mathematical formulas, identity proofs, coordinate geometry equations, and calculus shortcuts without requiring an internet connection.

### Core Features & Highlights

- **Algebra & Trigonometry**: Quadratic equations, complex numbers, sequences, series, and trigonometric identities.
- **Calculus**: Limits, continuity, differentiation, integration techniques, and differential equations.
- **Vector & 3D Geometry**: Vector products, line & plane equations, conic sections.
- **Offline & Eye-Friendly**: Optimized dark theme for continuous study sessions.
