## Who We Are

**NI18** is a technology entity founded by **Nikhil Sharma**, dedicated to building ultra-fast, maintainable, and user-centric software products.

Our core philosophy revolves around **engineering purity, zero fluff, and extreme performance**. Whether we are developing native Android applications with Jetpack Compose, architecting enterprise Salesforce Commerce Cloud (SFCC) storefronts, or creating privacy-first browser utilities, we adhere to strict quality standards.

### Our Core Pillars

1. **Performance First**: We target 100/100 Lighthouse scores and lightning-fast user interactions on every screen.
2. **Privacy & Security**: Client-side execution ensures user data remains in their control.
3. **Future-Proof Engineering**: Clean TypeScript, modular Kotlin, and zero server bloat.
